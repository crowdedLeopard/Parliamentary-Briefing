# Sources

## Review period

### Hansard (each sitting date)
Cover the Commons Chamber, Westminster Hall, Lords Chamber, Grand Committee, Public Bill Committees and oral questions.
- Commons overview: `https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/YYYY-MM-DD`
- Lords overview: `https://hansard.parliament.uk/Lords/YYYY-MM-DD`

Start from each day's overview page, then open each relevant debate or question page and link to that page, not the overview.

### Written questions, answers and statements
Use the pre-fetched file first (see `data-file.md`). Fall back to `https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/` and web search only if it is unavailable.

### Committees (Commons, Lords and Joint)
Use committees.parliament.uk (committee news, inquiry and publication pages), the Commons and Lords media notices on parliament.uk, and web searches such as `committees.parliament.uk inquiry [topic] [date]`.

Capture:
- new inquiries and calls for evidence, with the evidence deadline;
- oral evidence sessions held: who gave evidence, the key points, and the transcript link where published;
- reports published and government responses;
- notable published correspondence or written evidence.

Always check:
- Commons Science, Innovation and Technology
- Lords Science and Technology
- Lords Communications and Digital
- Commons Culture, Media and Sport
- Commons Business and Trade
- Commons Treasury
- Commons Public Accounts
- Commons Energy Security and Net Zero
- Commons Home Affairs
- Commons Public Administration and Constitutional Affairs
- Joint Committee on the National Security Strategy
- Joint Committee on Human Rights
- any special or ad hoc committee on AI, digital or technology

Example of the kind of item to catch: the Lords Science and Technology Committee inquiry "Future of UK compute in the age of AI", launched 23 November 2026 (https://committees.parliament.uk/work/10109).

### Libraries and POST
House of Commons Library, House of Lords Library and POST (Parliamentary Office of Science and Technology) publications from the period.

## Parliament Today (the day the briefing is sent)

- What's On: `https://whatson.parliament.uk/`. Check the Commons Chamber, Westminster Hall, Lords Chamber, Grand Committee and Committees tabs. Dated views are at `https://whatson.parliament.uk/commons/YYYY-MM-DD/` and `https://whatson.parliament.uk/lords/YYYY-MM-DD/`.
- Commons Order Paper: `https://commonsbusiness.parliament.uk/`
- Lords Business: `https://lordsbusiness.parliament.uk/`
- From these, cover:
  - departmental and topical oral questions, and Lords oral questions;
  - bill stages and Lords amendments;
  - Ten Minute Rule motions;
  - urgent questions or statements already announced;
  - Westminster Hall and adjournment debates.
- Committee meetings and evidence sessions scheduled today, and committee reports announced for publication today.
- Commons Library debate packs and Lords Library briefings for today's debates, and any POST or Library publications due today.

If a departmental question session is on today (for example DSIT, HMT, Cabinet Office, Home Office or DESNZ), name it and pick out any tabled questions on Microsoft's issues where visible.
