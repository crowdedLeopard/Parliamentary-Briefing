Government expects Microsoft to share lessons from the Microsoft 365 outage

Adam Dance (LD, Yeovil) to DCMS; answered by Ian Murray · Answered 23 Nov (UIN 43125)

Asked what assessment had been made of the cost to the UK economy of the 12–13 November Microsoft 365 outage, the minister said no assessment has been made because the impacts are still emerging. Why it matters. Microsoft was mentioned in a ministerial answer and on the Commons floor on 23 November, the day before the scheduled consideration of Lords amendments to the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill. The answer provides a basis for follow-up with DCMS on lessons learned and remediation.

The Unsafe Journeys policy was last updated in March 2026. The Government recognises the exceptionally difficult circumstances facing families in Gaza. Requests for biometric deferral and excusal are considered on a case-by-case basis, taking account of individual circumstances and relevant human rights obligations.
